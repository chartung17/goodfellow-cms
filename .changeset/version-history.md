Version history: pages, the header and footer, and collections' items have a **Version history** screen in the admin panel, listing every published version with who published it and when, a preview of each, and **Restore this version**, which publishes it again. Backends gain `history()` and `readAt()`, implemented for GitHub and GitLab and their fakes.
