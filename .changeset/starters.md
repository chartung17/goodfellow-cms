Add `create-goodfellow`, which creates a new site from the starter or the parish example (`npm create goodfellow@latest my-site`), and can set up where the site is stored and hosted. Sites can now keep their contact details (address, phone and email) in Site settings, shown wherever the new Contact details block is placed. A collection's link fields can choose an uploaded file, such as a PDF.
